Haulist Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Haulist is a shared shopping list for families. This page explains what it stores and why. Short version: we keep only what the app needs to work, we never sell it, and there are no ads.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and first name. If you use "Continue with Google", Google shares your name and email with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Your lists: categories, items, prices, store names and product links you add.
  • Family info: which family you belong to, and the "I'm shopping at…" status if you choose to share it.
  • Budget settings: monthly goals you enter, and (only if you connect it) the address of your own budget spreadsheet.

How it's used

Only to run Haulist: signing you in, showing your family the same list, and keeping budget totals. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Who can see it

Only members of your family in Haulist can see your family's lists. The database enforces this, so other users cannot read your data. Your data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, which keeps it encrypted in transit.

Deleting your data

You can remove any item or category at any time. To delete your account and everything tied to it, email the address below and it will be removed.

Children

Haulist isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
